Hi! I'm a student in the [M.S. in Computer Science](https://www.csd.cmu.edu/academics/masters/overview) program at Carnegie Mellon University's Kenneth C. Griffin School of Computer Science. I'm currently interested in backend systems and agentic workflows, from the infrastructure that makes AI systems reliable to their potential to improve how people work and live.

I also completed my undergraduate degrees at CMU in [BCSA](https://www.cmu.edu/interdisciplinary/programs/bcsa.html), an interdisciplinary program blending Computer Science and Architecture, and in [Cognitive Science](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/psychology/undergraduate/prospective-students/academics/cognitive-science/index.html) through the Department of Psychology. BCSA gave me a foundation at the intersection of computation and design, while Cognitive Science let me dive into how people think, perceive, and interact with technology, bridging insights from psychology, neuroscience, and artificial intelligence.
