## The problem

A database engine needs to move pages between disk and memory, find records efficiently, and execute queries over them. My work covers the first three BusTub projects: the buffer pool manager, B+ tree index, and query execution.

## My approach

I worked through these layers in sequence, from managing pages to indexing them and then processing queries.
